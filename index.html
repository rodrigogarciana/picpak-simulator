PicPak Preview

4-color e-ink simulator

About this preview

PicPak Preview helps you adjust images before sending them to a PicPak by approximating the 4-color screen from the Kickstarter project. It is a work in progress and does not fully mimic the real device yet, but it tries to provide a useful preview. No image data is sent to any server. Uploads, adjustments, and rendering all happen directly in your browser on the client side.
